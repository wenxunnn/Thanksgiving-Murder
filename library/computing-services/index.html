Network access: a short guide

University systems use internal addresses such as 10.42.x.x. Connections to the public internet may pass through a university gateway, such as 128.46.x.x. A server address identifies the system providing a service.

Authorized readers and researchers can also connect remotely through the university’s VPN service. Session records identify an account, the service reached, and the source address observed for the connection.

An account login and a physical entrance record are different records. A network address alone does not identify a person’s physical location.
