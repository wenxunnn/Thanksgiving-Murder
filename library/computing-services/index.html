Remote Access Guide
(2008)

Published 14 August 2008 · Laboratory systems support

This guide describes routine connection records for laboratory computer systems and the university's remote-access service.

Remote laboratory access

Remote access to university laboratory systems is available through the WLU VPN gateway. Successful remote authentication does not establish physical presence at a campus workstation.

A VPN session may retain the external source address observed at the gateway before the tunnel is established. An internal address assigned inside the tunnel is a different field. Read the source address, connection type, and destination together when requesting technical support.

Understanding an access entry

Laboratory instrument clients record a username, destination system, source address, connection type, and authentication result. “Access granted” confirms that the service accepted credentials. Shared terminals, external gateways, and remote sessions may produce different address formats.

Private campus addresses describe an internal network. Public university addresses describe the university's outward-facing network allocation. Other public source addresses may belong to external connections; an address alone cannot establish a person's exact physical location or who operated a client.
