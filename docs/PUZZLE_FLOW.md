## Nonlinear investigation

Library, hospital and telephone sources may be investigated in different orders. CCTV is physically discovered on the balcony, not password-gated again. The briefcase cannot contain the only library credential or only Nokia reference. The Dean card includes the phone-terminal URL and must be in the living room so that the phone keypad can serve as a reference before the briefcase is open. A copied web address is not proof of physical access or an authenticated person’s location.

No single correspondence item, alibi, witness observation or clinical finding is an automated accusation. Compare motive, opportunity, glass identification, toxicology and time windows. Camera snapshots describe instants, not a continuous recording or exact death clock.
