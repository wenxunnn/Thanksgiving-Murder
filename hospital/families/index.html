Patient & Family Services

Practical support
through difficult times.

A serious illness affects the whole family. Our family liaison office helps parents organize hospital correspondence, find the right departmental contact, and prepare for scheduled care conferences.

Family financial assistance

Families may request an assessment for financial assistance or discuss payment arrangements with the billing office. Assistance depends on the information supplied with an application and does not automatically change an existing statement balance.

Keep copies of statements and correspondence. Your account reference allows staff to locate the correct billing record without relying on a patient's name alone.

Questions about a statement
