Patient Portal
Security & Privacy

This reference explains the information recorded when an account is accessed and the limits of interpreting a technical access record.

Website access records

For security purposes, the patient portal records the public IP address from which an account is accessed. The originating IP address is not necessarily associated with the physical location of a person, particularly when remote access or institutional networks are involved.

An address may represent a household internet connection, an organization's shared gateway, a proxy, or the public endpoint of a remote connection. A single address may be used by more than one person.

Authentication and remote connections

Authenticated access indicates that valid credentials were accepted by a service. It does not, by itself, identify the individual at the keyboard or establish physical presence at a particular location. Remote authentication and an in-person workstation session should not be treated as equivalent records.

Technical help: private and public addresses

Private network examples:

  • 10.x.x.x
  • 172.16.x.x–172.31.x.x
  • 192.168.x.x

These ranges are reserved for private networks and are not directly routed on the public internet.

Public addresses may belong to home internet connections, organizations, or remote-access gateways. Their appearance in a log is not a precise location measurement.

Keeping an account reference safe

Keep statement correspondence with your family records. If you have a question about an activity entry, contact the billing office and quote the account reference and the date of the entry.
