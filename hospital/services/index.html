Children's Services

Pediatric care & coordination

St. Agnes brings together children's outpatient clinics, inpatient services, and family liaison support. Each child has a named point of contact for appointment correspondence.

Pediatric oncology services

The pediatric oncology office coordinates specialist consultations, scheduled treatment visits, laboratory appointments, and family care conferences. Parents may ask the family liaison office for help organizing their questions and correspondence.

For account or statement questions relating to an oncology visit, contact Patient Billing & Family Services using the account reference on your statement.
