Examination findings

External injury · Chest

A penetrating stab wound of the chest is present. Examination findings are consistent with the wound having been inflicted after death. This postmortem injury was not the cause of death.

Toxicological examination

Toxicological analysis detected cyanide in specimens collected at postmortem examination. The findings, considered with the examination and investigative information, support acute cyanide poisoning as the cause of death.

Associated evidence · Item GL-17-A

The submitted gold-rimmed drinking glass, identified as item GL-17-A, was examined for associated chemical residue. Cyanide residue was detected. This finding concerns the submitted item and does not identify the person responsible for its contamination.

Summary of medical opinion

Death resulted from acute cyanide poisoning. The chest stab wound was inflicted after death and did not cause the fatality. The manner of death is homicide. The approximately 10:24 PM time is an investigative estimate subject to the limitations described above.
