LARK INVESTIGATION SERVICES · WITNESS RECORD

Case TC-08-1127-017 · Recorded November 28, 2008

Statement of Sophie Bennett

At about 9:42 PM on Thanksgiving evening, I saw Dr. Victor Lin handling Professor Whitmore's distinctive gold-rimmed drinking glass. He lifted it and set it down again. I could not tell whether anything was put into it.

I said I was leaving for the library at around 9:35 PM. I came back briefly at about 9:55 PM to retrieve belongings. Later, Daniel and I boarded the 10:35 PM shuttle together.

I realized I had left my ukulele bag behind. I tried calling Daniel at 11:08 PM, but he did not answer. I took a later shuttle back, arrived near Lark at approximately 11:30 PM, and reached the townhouse at about 11:32 PM.

At approximately 11:35 PM, I found Professor Whitmore slumped in his chair on the first-floor balcony. There was a knife in his chest. He did not respond. I raised the alarm and called the police at around 11:42 PM.

This statement records what I recall seeing. I cannot account for everything that happened while I was elsewhere.

Sophie Bennett
