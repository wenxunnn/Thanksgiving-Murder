Statement of Sophie Bennett

Hello? This is Sophie Bennett.

He was slumped in his chair, with a knife sticking out of his chest. I thought he might still be alive, but he wasn't responding.

I was terrified. I called the police at 11:42.

I don't know what happened to him. I really don't.
