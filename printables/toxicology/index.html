Confirmatory laboratory findings identify cyanide exposure. Residue recovered from the interior of the distinctive gold-rimmed drinking glass, item GL-17-A, is consistent with the same contaminant identified in the toxicology examination.

The cause of death is cyanide poisoning. The medical estimate places death at approximately 10:24 PM EST. This is an estimate derived from the medical examination and case findings, not an exact time established by a surveillance photograph.

The wound findings are consistent with an injury inflicted after death. The chest wound did not cause the fatal collapse.

Interpretation

These findings concern cause, timing, and the identified glass. They do not identify who introduced a contaminant or establish that person's intent.

pH indicator paper measures acidity/alkalinity and is not a test for cyanide. The cyanide finding above is based on laboratory toxicology.
